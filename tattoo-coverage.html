Care, removal & sensitive skin.

Care

Treat it gently and it will last all day. Avoid harsh rubbing or scrubbing, oils and oil-based products, lotions and soaps on the area, and tight clothing that creates friction.

Removal

It comes off easily at the end of the day with any oil-based cleanser or oil-based makeup remover.

Sensitive skin?

Have sensitive skin or allergies? Tell me when you inquire and we'll plan around it.
