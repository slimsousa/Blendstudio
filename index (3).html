About Me

A passion for making
brides feel seen.

With 25 years of professional makeup artistry experience, I bring both depth of training and a genuine love for the craft to every client I work with. A graduate of the David Nicholas Makeup Academy and Dinair-certified in airbrush application, I've spent over two decades refining my technique — and honestly, I still get excited every single time a bride sees herself in the mirror.

My work spans an extensive range of premium brands — Bobbi Brown, Lancôme, Chanel, NARS, Dior, M.A.C, Clinique, and Estée Lauder — giving me the knowledge to find exactly what works for your skin, your features, and your vision. I stay current on every new trend and technique, because I believe you should never have to choose between timeless and fresh. You deserve both.

Having completed over 100 bridal looks throughout my career, I understand the trust a bride places in her makeup artist — and I don't take that lightly. My goal is simple: to make you feel like the most beautiful, confident version of yourself on the day that matters most.

Services & Pricing

Everything you need
for your perfect day.

01

Bridal Makeup

Your wedding day deserves nothing less than perfection. I dedicate a full, uninterrupted hour exclusively to you — ensuring flawless skin, seamless blending, and a look that photographs beautifully in every kind of light. I highly recommend pairing your bridal makeup with a trial session 4–8 weeks before the big day. It's the perfect opportunity to explore your ideal look and decide whether strip lashes or cluster lashes suit you best. Lash application is something I truly pride myself on, and I want every bride to feel confident and stunning from every angle.

Bride $200  ·  Trial $125

02

Bridal Party

Your bridal party, your rules. Some brides want a cohesive, coordinated look across the whole party — others want each bridesmaid to bring their own personality to the chair. Either way, I've got you covered. Every application is thoughtfully tailored to the individual while keeping the overall look of the day in harmony.

From $150 / person

03

Trial Session

A 90-minute trial session scheduled 4–8 weeks before your wedding gives us the time and space to get everything just right. We'll explore your ideal look together, address any skin concerns, and make sure every detail feels perfectly you. We'll also take the time to decide between strip lashes or cluster lashes — because the right lashes can elevate the entire look, and I want you to feel confident in that choice before the big day. We'll also go over a basic skincare routine to ensure your complexion is healthy, luminous, and camera-ready when it matters most. Consider it your dress rehearsal for the most important look of your life.

$125 / 90 minute session

✦ Travel within the South Shore is complimentary. Extended travel available — please inquire. All prices are estimates; a custom quote will be provided.

✦ If you"re looking for hairstyling as well, I'm happy to share the contact information for trusted bridal hairstylists I regularly work with.

The Experience

What to expect
when you book with Blend.

01

Inquire

Fill out the inquiry form with your wedding date, location, and any details you have so far. Once I receive it, I'll review everything and be in touch to learn more about your wedding day and your makeup needs.

02

Connect

Once your inquiry comes through, expect a personal email from me within 48 hours. I'll be in touch to go over the details of your wedding day, your makeup vision, and any questions you may have. If you dont see a reply, be sure to check your spam folder just in case.

03

Your Trial

If you choose to book a bridal trial, we'll schedule it closer to your wedding date to refine your look and make sure everything feels just right. It's also a chance for us to make any adjustments so you feel confident and comfortable before the big day.

04

Wedding Day

As your wedding day approaches, I'll confirm your timeline and final details so the morning feels smooth, calm, and well organized. On the day itself, Ill arrive on-site ready to bring your vision to life.
